Our Story

We've Been
Where You Are.

Life after 30 doesn't slow down — it accelerates. Careers, families, and responsibilities pile up while the hours in the day stay exactly the same. We built FitAfter30 because we lived it ourselves — juggling demanding schedules while watching our energy, strength, and confidence quietly erode. We tried every program out there and found the same problem: they were all built for people with unlimited time and zero obligations.

So we built what we wished had existed. FitAfter30 is a community, a toolkit, and a plan — all in one. Every program is designed around 30 minutes because that's what real life allows. Whether you're just getting started or getting back on track after years away, you belong here.
